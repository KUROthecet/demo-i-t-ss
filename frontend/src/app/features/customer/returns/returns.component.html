Returns & Refunds

We'll make it right

Clear process, honest timelines, no runaround. Cancellations are easy as long as your order hasn't been approved yet.

01

Cancel Before Approval

Access your order through the Orders page, enter your email, and confirm the cancellation. As long as a product manager hasn't approved it yet, you're free to cancel — no questions asked.

02

Refund by Method

PayPal: Refunds process automatically within 3–5 business days when an order is cancelled or rejected.

VietQR: Our team contacts you to arrange a manual bank transfer. Allow 1–3 business days after cancellation.

03

After Approval

Once an order is approved, it cannot be cancelled through the platform. If you receive a damaged or incorrect item, contact our support team and we will assess your case individually — every situation is different.

What we don't cover

  • Items used or opened without a defect
  • Returns without contacting support first
  • Digital goods or non-physical media

Need help?

We respond within one business day. Include your order reference and we'll sort it out.
