The AIMS Journey

It started with a simple question: in a world moving purely to digital streams, what happens to the things we can touch? The books we flip through, the CDs we treasure, the physical media that defines our memories.

AIMS was born out of that question. We didn't want to build just another storefront; we wanted to create a seamless bridge between the past and the future. By eliminating mandatory logins and introducing blazing-fast 2-second checkouts via PayPal and VietQR, we set out to make owning physical media feel effortless.

For the managers behind the scenes, we engineered a system capable of running 300 hours non-stop, handling thousands of simultaneous users without breaking a sweat. From automatic inventory updates to secure refund protocols, every line of code was crafted to ensure total command over the platform.

Today, AIMS is more than a capstone project. It’s an unwavering commitment to the tangible, a platform where cutting-edge technology empowers the joy of physical ownership. Welcome to our story.
